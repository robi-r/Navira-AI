FAQ

Straight answers.

How accurate are the numbers?

They're estimates for planning and pricing, not quotes. Freight, duty and tax rates come from illustrative tables, and every line says how it was worked out. Confirm with your carrier and customs broker before you book.

Which Incoterms are supported?

EXW, FCA, FOB, CFR, CIF, CPT, CIP, DAP and DDP. The estimate only includes the freight, insurance, duty and tax that the importer pays under the term you choose.

Does it work for sea, air and road?

Yes. Air and road use chargeable weight (the greater of actual and volumetric). Sea uses the weight-or-measure rule per cubic metre.

Can it handle preferential origin?

Tick "Claim preferential origin" and duty drops to the preferential rate. The checklist then makes the certificate of origin a required item.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Navira AI is open source. Clone it from GitHub and run it locally in a couple of commands.
